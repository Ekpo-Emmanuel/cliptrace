why cliptrace

Win+V forgets.
Cliptrace remembers.

Windows keeps your last 25 copies and clears them when you restart. Cliptrace keeps what you copy, finds it by meaning, and never leaves your PC.

Pins sit above the trace and never expire. Your address or sign-off is always at the top of quick paste, one keystroke away.

hide

Secrets stay hidden.

API keys and tokens are caught as you copy them, and you can mark any clip as secret. Encrypted on your device, masked everywhere and left out of search. Password manager copies are never saved.

undo

Nothing is gone for five seconds.

Delete one clip or thirty. You get five seconds to take it back. Pause capture from the tray when you would rather not keep a copy.

Download for Windows All downloads
  1. 01 Run the installer Cliptrace-setup.exe, or the portable zip from All downloads.
  2. 02 If Windows warns you The app isn’t code signed yet. Choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  3. 03 First run A one-time download of the search model. After that, nothing leaves your PC.
  4. 04 Press CtrlAltV Copy as usual. Your trace starts now.

Cliptrace is open source. Read the code, report a bug, or send a fix. If it saves you time, a star on GitHub helps other people find it.
